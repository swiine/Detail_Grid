ET WALLS (STANDALONE) - INSTRUCTIONS
====================================
Engineering Technologies

A Windows app that designs a retaining wall, prices it, draws the section and writes the
calculation report. No Civil 3D needed, nothing to install.
Keep it in the "Standalone Tool" folder: reports, DXFs and saved designs go into
"_Exports\date\job number\wall name", e.g. _Exports\2026-10-28\241001\W1 - Carpark east\
(job number and wall name are entered in Design step 1).
The company CAD standard comes from "..\_support\_Standards" (TTW.json).
Files you put straight into "_Exports" are moved into their date folder when the app starts.


USE IT
------
1. Double-click  "ET Walls.exe".
2. Design tab: follow the 4 steps (Next / Back at the bottom).
3. On the result page click "Draw this wall", pick a scale, then "Save DXF...".
4. In Civil 3D / AutoCAD: INSERT the DXF (or drag the file into the drawing). It is drawn
   in metres at true size, on the CAD standard's layers.


DESIGN
------
  1  Project   Project, job no., wall name, designed / checked by. Pick the wall type:
               cantilever concrete, block (gravity), mass gravity, or sleeper and post.
  2  Wall      Only the retained height is needed: the wall is sized for you (the cheapest
               size that passes every check). Also: backfill slope, wall length (for the
               total cost), cut or fill site, footing under the soil (heel) or in front only
               (no heel, e.g. on a boundary), and which way the drawing faces.
               "Custom wall size" lets you set every size yourself.
  3  Soil &    Backfill and foundation soil (quick picks), bearing, surcharge, drainage /
     loads     water, earthquake. Concrete, cover and bar size (cantilever).
               Spare capacity: keep every check below 95%, 90% or 85% if you want margin.
               "Rates for the cost estimate": concrete, steel, formwork, digging and so on.
               "Save as my rates" makes them the starting rates for every new design.
  4  Result    Every check (with the working), the bar schedule, the cost estimate, the
               options that pass (cheapest, narrowest base, least steel, most spare) with
               "Use this", and "what if" tables.

  The result updates as you type. Type numbers any way: 2.4 or 2400mm, 30°, 10kPa.
  A box turns red and says why if it can't be read.

  On the result page:
    Draw this wall        -> the Detail tab (scale, standard, Save DXF)
    Calculation report... -> saves the report twice, side by side, and opens it:
                             .html  opens in your browser (print it to PDF)
                             .xlsx  Excel: summary, inputs, checks, reinforcement,
                                    cost (change a rate and the totals update), what if
    Save design...        -> reopen later with "Open a saved design..." (step 1)


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- The "Used" percent is how much of each check's capacity is used. Under 100% passes.
- The method is a placeholder (Rankine earth pressure, factors of safety, AS 3600-style
  reinforcement, Broms for posts) until the company spreadsheets are added. Costs use
  placeholder rates: put in your own. An engineer must check and sign off every result.
- Global (overall slope) stability is not checked: use Slide or Geo5 where it matters.
- Picture: scroll to zoom, drag to move, double-click to fit.

If Windows says .NET is missing: install the ".NET 8 Desktop Runtime" (x64) from
microsoft.com. Civil 3D 2026 machines already have it.
