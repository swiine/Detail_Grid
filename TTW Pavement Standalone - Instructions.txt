TTW PAVEMENT (STANDALONE) - INSTRUCTIONS
========================================

A small Windows app. No Civil 3D needed, nothing to install.
Everything you save goes to the shared exports folder:
   A:\Civil\AutoCAD\Global\Australia\NSW\_default\Scripts\TTW_Pvmt_Tool\Standalone Tool\_Exports
in "date\job number\pavement name", e.g.
   _Exports\2026-10-13\241001\Type A - Carpark\
If the A: drive isn't connected, it saves to the local "_Exports" folder next
to the app instead (the status line says so).
(job number and pavement name are entered in Design step 1),
and the company standards come from "..\_support\_Standards".
Files you put straight into "_Exports" are moved into their date folder when
the app starts.


USE IT
------
1. Double-click  "TTW Pavement.exe".
2. Paste or type the build-up (any wording).
3. Check the picture.
4. Click "Save DXF..." (for CAD) or "Save PDF..." (to email - to scale when
   it fits the paper; pick A4 or A3 under Drawing > PDF paper).

Then in Civil 3D / AutoCAD: INSERT the DXF (or drag the file into the drawing).

MANY DETAILS AT ONCE: "Batch from Excel..."
  1. "Make the Excel template..." and fill it in: one pavement per row,
     a Name, then the layers (Layer 1, Layer 2...) or the whole build-up in
     one cell, plus the subgrade.
  2. "Open Excel list..." - red rows can't be read (fix, save, "Read again").
  3. "Save all as one DXF..." (each detail is a block, in a grid) or
     "Save all as PDF..." (one sheet per detail).

"Copy for TTWPVMT" copies the build-up so you can paste it into
TTWPVMT > New pavement detail instead.


DESIGN (CALCULATIONS FOR ENGINEERS)
-----------------------------------
Click the "Design" tab and follow the 4 steps (Next / Back at the bottom):

  1  Project   Job name, number, pavement name, designed / checked by.
               Pick ROAD / CAR PARK (asphalt over crushed rock)
               or CONCRETE SLAB (warehouse, hardstand, loading dock).
  2  Ground    Subgrade CBR (quick buttons for typical soils).
               Slab: soil depth; Es is worked out from the CBR.
  3  Loads     Road: pick the design traffic (ESA), or work it out from
               traffic counts (AADT, % trucks, growth, life).
               Slab: tick forklifts/trucks, racks and/or storage and fill
               them in. Advanced factors (k1, k3 ...) are under a link.
  4  Result    The answer, every check, "what if" tables (CBR, traffic,
               concrete grade) and a picture of the build-up.

  The result line at the bottom updates live as you type.
  Type numbers any way: 20t or 196kN, 1e6 or 1 x 10^6, 100,000, 5%.
  A box turns red and says why if it can't be read.

  On the result page:
    Draw this build-up    -> sends it to the Detail tab (then Save DXF)
    Calculation report... -> saves the report twice, side by side:
                             .html  opens in your browser (print it to PDF)
                             .xlsx  Excel workbook: summary, what-if, one sheet
                                    per check (numbers you can reuse)
                             Each links to the other.
    Save design...        -> reopen later with "Open a saved design..."
                             (the report saves the design next to it too)
    Project register...   -> see below
    Rates...              -> the rough-cost rates (see below)

  PROJECT REGISTER (step 1 or the result page)
    Every design saved for the job number, from any day: result, thickness,
    rough cost, report. Double-click a row to open its report.
    Select 2 or more rows (Ctrl+click) > "Compare selected..." for an options
    report (HTML + Excel): build-ups side by side, thickness, what governs,
    cost, and the thinnest / cheapest option.
    "Export register to Excel..." saves the list with links to the reports.

  ROUGH COST
    Rates per layer live in _support\_Standards\TTW_Rates.json (shared by the
    team). THE RATES SHIPPED ARE PLACEHOLDERS - put real rates in before using
    the costs. Enter the pavement area in step 1 for a total.
    A layer with no rate is flagged on the result.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Thickness in mm. The DXF is drawn in metres (change it under Drawing > Units).
- [ ... ] = the title,  ( ... ) = a note on the label,  2 x 150mm = two layers.
- Scale: pick 1:5, 1:10, 1:20 ... from the list (or type one).
- CAD standard: TTW.json from "_support\_Standards" is used by default.
  Pick another in the list, or "Standards folder..." for another folder
  (e.g. on the shared drive). It remembers your choice.
- Picture: scroll to zoom, drag to move, double-click to fit.
- Break lines are simple Z breaks (the TTW break block is only added inside Civil 3D).
- Can't read it? Click "Ask AI (free)...".

TIME SAVED (for management)
---------------------------
"Time saved..." (Detail tab) shows hours saved by tool, month and person.
Every use is logged on your own PC (%APPDATA%\PavementBuildup\usage.jsonl;
tool, date and login only - shared by the Civil 3D tool and this app).
For the whole team: everyone sends you their usage.jsonl, then
"Add other people's logs..." and "Save report (HTML + Excel)...".
Minutes saved per use: "Minutes per tool..." (TTW_TimeSaved.json in
_support\_Standards) - set them to what the team agrees.

TRAINING
--------
Double-click  "TTW Pavement - Training.cmd"  (next to the app).
It opens the app in practice mode with a step list on the right that ticks
itself as you go. Files go to a temporary folder; nothing is counted.

MY SETTINGS (the cog, top right of every window)

Dark mode is the default. Click the cog to change, just for you:
- Theme: Dark or Light (changes straight away)
- Your name: fills "Designed by" when it's blank
- Default detail scale (e.g. 1:10)
- Text size: Normal / Larger / Largest (windows opened after saving)
- Count my tool use in the time saved report
Saved in %APPDATA%\PavementBuildup\user.json ("Open my settings folder").

REPORT A PROBLEM
----------------
"Report a problem..." (Detail tab) saves a zip on your Desktop with the
error log, settings and a screenshot of the app. If the app hits an error
it offers this by itself.

NEW VERSIONS
------------
When _support\update.json names the network release folder, the app tells
you (a bar at the top) when a newer version is there.

If Windows says .NET is missing: install the ".NET 8 Desktop Runtime" (x64)
from microsoft.com. Civil 3D 2026 machines already have it.
