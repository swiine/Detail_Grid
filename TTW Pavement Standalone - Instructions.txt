TTW PAVEMENT (STANDALONE) - INSTRUCTIONS
========================================

A small Windows app. No Civil 3D needed, nothing to install.


USE IT
------
1. Double-click  "TTW Pavement.exe".
2. Paste or type the build-up (any wording).
3. Check the picture.
4. Click "Save DXF...".

Then in Civil 3D / AutoCAD: INSERT the DXF (or drag the file into the drawing).

"Copy for TTWPVMT" copies the build-up so you can paste it into
TTWPVMT > New pavement detail instead.


DESIGN (CALCULATIONS FOR ENGINEERS)
-----------------------------------
Click the "Design (calculations)" tab.
  1. Choose a calculation on the left.
  2. Fill in the inputs (hover over a box for a hint). Results update as you type.
  3. Read the result. Green = OK, red = no good.

  Flexible pavement - Austroads (granular, thin AC)
      CBR + design traffic (ESA) -> AC / DGB20 / DGS20 thicknesses from the
      company design table, checked against Austroads Fig 8.4 / Fig 12.2.
      "Draw this build-up" sends it to the Detail tab in the spec wording.
  Concrete (T48): wheels interior / edge, posts interior / edge, punching
      shear, UDL thickness, UDL deflection.
  Concrete (T34): wheel stress check - Westergaard / Ioannides / Pickett
      (also gives the least thickness that passes).
  Soil: CBR -> k -> Es, and equivalent soil modulus for up to 4 layers.

  "Copy calculation" / "Save calculation..." give the full worked sheet
  (inputs, every step, result, notes) for the design report.
  Same formulas as the company spreadsheets - always check the results.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Thickness in mm. The DXF is drawn in metres (change it under Drawing > Units).
- [ ... ] = the title,  ( ... ) = a note on the label,  2 x 150mm = two layers.
- Scale: pick 1:5, 1:10, 1:20 ... from the list (or type one).
- CAD standard: click "Standards folder..." and pick the company folder
  (e.g. on the shared drive). Every standard in it shows in the list.
  It remembers the folder and your choice.
- Picture: scroll to zoom, drag to move, double-click to fit.
- Break lines are simple Z breaks (the TTW break block is only added inside Civil 3D).
- Can't read it? Click "Ask AI (free)...".

If Windows says .NET is missing: install the ".NET 8 Desktop Runtime" (x64)
from microsoft.com. Civil 3D 2026 machines already have it.
