1 · Toolspace-style: tabs down the side

Works like Toolspace: the tabs run down the edge (Menu, Chat, Friends, Drafting). The Menu tab is a tree you can arrange yourself: folders, your own commands, and right-click to add, rename or reorder. This is the closest match to what you already use.

2 · Properties-style: sections that fold up

Laid out like the Properties panel: sections you can fold up, with a name on the left and a value or button on the right. It's packed with information (who's on, your current room, the drafting status) and your custom commands get their own section.

CoWork⇔ ✕

3 · Tool Palette tiles

Big buttons in groups, like AutoCAD's Tool Palettes. Each tile is one command. Drag tiles to reorder them, and use the dashed "+" tile to add your own. The easiest of the five to click and to customise.

CoWork⇔ ✕

4 · Slim icon strip that opens out

Takes almost no screen space: a 46-pixel strip of icons docked to the edge. Click an icon and its panel slides out (Friends is open here), then it tucks away again, like auto-hide. Red badges show unread messages. Your own commands sit at the bottom of the strip.

5 · All in one: quick buttons above the chat

One panel does everything: a row of your custom command buttons at the top, friends' faces underneath (click one to message them), then the chat. You don't need to switch tabs, so it suits people who keep the chat open all day.
