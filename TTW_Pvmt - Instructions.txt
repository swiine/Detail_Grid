TTW PAVEMENT - INSTRUCTIONS
===========================

Draws a pavement build-up detail in Civil 3D 2026 from any written build-up.


INSTALL (once)
--------------
1. Close Civil 3D.
2. Copy the "PavementBuildup.bundle" folder into:
      %APPDATA%\Autodesk\ApplicationPlugins
   (Updating? Delete the old folder there first.)
3. Open Civil 3D.


USE IT
------
Type  TTWPVMT  and pick what you want to do.
"Back to menu" (or Esc while clicking in the drawing) takes you back.

  1  New pavement detail
       - Paste or type the build-up (any wording).
       - Check the picture.
       - Click "Place in drawing", then click where it goes.

  2  Edit a detail in the drawing
       - Click the detail, change it, click "Update detail".

  3  Detail from a note in the drawing
       - Click the text note, check the picture, place it.

  4  Quick place
       - Type the build-up on the command line, then click where it goes.
       - Shortcut: type  PAVEQUICK

  5  CAD standard
       - Layers, hatches, text sizes, break lines.
       - Tip: "Copy from drawing..." copies a hatch or layer you already like.


HOW TO WRITE A BUILD-UP
-----------------------
Top to bottom, thickness in mm. Write it however you like, for example:

   60mm pavers, 30mm sand, 150mm road base, subgrade

   [CARPARK PAVEMENT]  40mm asphalt, 2 x 150mm road base, subgrade

   [ ... ]   = the title
   ( ... )   = a note on the label only
   2 x 150mm = two layers of 150mm


IF IT CAN'T READ YOUR BUILD-UP
------------------------------
Click "Ask AI (free)..." in the window.
  1. Claude.ai opens in your browser (free login). Press Send.
  2. Copy Claude's reply.
  3. The tool fills itself in.

Or open "AI Pavement Build-up Prompt.txt", paste it into any AI chat with
your build-up, and paste the reply into the tool.

Paid AI (optional, you don't need it): type  TTWPVMTAI
