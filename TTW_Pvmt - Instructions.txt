TTW PAVEMENT - INSTRUCTIONS
===========================

Draws a pavement build-up detail in Civil 3D 2026 from any written build-up.


INSTALL (once)
--------------
1. Close Civil 3D.
2. Copy the "PavementBuildup.bundle" folder (in this "Civil3D Tool" folder) into:
      %APPDATA%\Autodesk\ApplicationPlugins
   (Updating? Delete the old folder there first.)
3. Open Civil 3D.
4. It uses the company standard TTW.json (_support\_Standards) by default.
   To use another one: New pavement detail > More options > Use another...
   "Use TTW default" goes back to TTW.json.


USE IT
------
Type  TTWPVMT  and pick what you want to do.
"Back to menu" (or Esc while clicking in the drawing) takes you back.

  1  New pavement detail
       - Paste or type the build-up (any wording).
       - Check the picture.
       - Click "Place in drawing", then click where it goes.

  2  Edit a detail in the drawing
       - Click the detail, change it, click "Update detail".

  3  Detail from a note in the drawing
       - Click the text note, check the picture, place it.

  4  Quick place
       - Type the build-up on the command line, then click where it goes.
       - Shortcut: type  PAVEQUICK

  5  Arrange details on a sheet
       - Click the sheet frame (closed polyline / block), or Enter and pick
         two corners. Then select the details.
       - They're packed in rows, biggest first, with no overlaps. Any that
         don't fit go below the sheet (use a bigger or second sheet).

  6  CAD standard
       - Layers, hatches, text sizes, break lines.
       - Tip: "Copy from drawing..." copies a hatch or layer you already like.

  7  Time saved report
       - Opens a report of how much the tools have saved you (hours by
         tool and month). Minutes per use: _support\_Standards\TTW_TimeSaved.json.
       - Each use is logged on your PC: %APPDATA%\PavementBuildup\usage.jsonl
         (tool, date, your login - nothing from the drawing).


REPORT A PROBLEM / TRAINING
---------------------------
"Report a problem..." (bottom of the TTWPVMT menu): describe what happened;
it saves a zip on your Desktop (error log, settings, screenshot) to send.
Errors are also logged automatically: %APPDATA%\PavementBuildup\errors.log

TRAINING: type  TTW_PVMT_TRAINING
  Opens a practice drawing (temp folder) with a sample note and sheet,
  and a step-by-step list. Close it without saving after.
  Training use isn't counted in the time saved report.


HOW TO WRITE A BUILD-UP
-----------------------
Top to bottom, thickness in mm. Write it however you like, for example:

   60mm pavers, 30mm sand, 150mm road base, subgrade

   [CARPARK PAVEMENT]  40mm asphalt, 2 x 150mm road base, subgrade

   [ ... ]   = the title
   ( ... )   = a note on the label only
   2 x 150mm = two layers of 150mm


IF IT CAN'T READ YOUR BUILD-UP
------------------------------
Click "Ask AI (free)..." in the window.
  1. Claude.ai opens in your browser (free login). Press Send.
  2. Copy Claude's reply.
  3. The tool fills itself in.

Or open "_support\AI Pavement Build-up Prompt.txt", paste it into any AI chat with
your build-up, and paste the reply into the tool.

Paid AI (optional, you don't need it): type  TTWPVMTAI
