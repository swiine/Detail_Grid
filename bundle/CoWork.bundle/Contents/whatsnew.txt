1.2.0
- Something went wrong? Settings now has a "Send problem report" box - your report and CoWork's log go straight to support.

- CoWork tells you when someone else has the drawing you're in open, with Message and Ask to CoWork buttons.
- Hosts can hand the session to someone else, remove a guest, or make guests view-only.
- Hosting now autosaves every 5 minutes (change it in Settings).
