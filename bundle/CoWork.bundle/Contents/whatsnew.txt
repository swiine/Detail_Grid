1.4.3
- Session servers: work on PCs with IPv6 turned off and with guests that look the host up either way; large changes are passed on faster.
- CWCHECK tests the real connection to the session server (and office server) and says why it fails - usually the host PC's firewall. Allow-CoWork-Sessions.cmd (in the CoWork folder) fixes that: run it once as admin on each PC that hosts, or ask IT.
- Settings no longer says "Shared folder only" when you simply haven't set up the optional office server.

1.4.2
- Presenter mode: the host clicks "Present to everyone" on the Session page (or CWPRESENT) and everyone's view follows theirs - great for design reviews. Pan or zoom to look around; "Rejoin presentation" takes you back.
- Time on job (Settings, off unless you turn it on): counts the minutes you're working in each job's drawings for your timesheet. It stays on your PC only. CWTIME shows today and this week; Export saves a CSV.

1.4.1
- Fixed: opening CoWork (CWCHAT / CW) could crash if your licence was confirmed after Civil 3D had started.
- Updates no longer depend on the network drive being ready the moment Civil 3D starts: CoWork also checks while it's running, copies the new version to your PC and tells you when it's ready (restart Civil 3D to use it).
- CWCHECK now shows which version you're on, which is published, and what happened at the last start.

1.4.0
- Faster CoWork: when you start a session, your Civil 3D runs a small server for it automatically, so changes, crosshairs and locks go straight between PCs (the Session page shows the speed). If it can't connect, the shared folder still works.
- More things sync: text styles, dimension styles, linetypes, block definitions (BEDIT), paper space and layouts, pipe networks (moving pipes and structures, rim and sump) and sample lines (sections follow).
- Follow mode: on the Session page, Follow someone and your view follows their zoom and pan (or CWFOLLOW).
- Invite anyone to CoWork from their name or friend card; friends' sessions show the drawing and host, with Join and Watch.
- Chat: react to messages, "Seen" in private chats, and "is typing..." (with the server).
- CWCHECK (or Settings > Run health check) checks your folder, clock, server and licence.

1.3.1
- Safety copies: when a CoWork session starts, every 30 minutes while you host, and before a hand-over, CoWork keeps a copy of the drawing in the job's civil\_backup folder (e.g. 241796\civil\_backup). The Session page lists them with Open and Restore.
- Fixed: CoWork no longer asks for your licence key every time Civil 3D starts.

1.3.0
- Server mode (optional): if your office runs the CoWork server, chat, who's online and CoWork sessions update instantly, and other people's crosshairs move smoothly. Settings > Server shows whether you're connected. Without the server everything works through the shared folder as before.

1.2.3
- Fixed: handing over hosting no longer disconnects everyone. Guests wait while the new host opens the drawing, and the old host joins back in as a guest automatically.

1.2.2
- Right-click a message to edit or delete it (your own), or pin it to the top of the chat (anyone's).
- Pinned messages sit at the top of each chat - great for the levels datum or client instructions.
- Do not disturb: Friends page (or CWBUSY) - pauses all pop-ups for an hour or until tomorrow; friends see you're busy.

1.2.0
- Something went wrong? Settings now has a "Send problem report" box - your report and CoWork's log go straight to support.
- Share a view: the pin button next to Send posts "look here"; clicking it opens the same drawing at the same spot with the objects selected.
- CoWork tells you when someone else has the drawing you're in open, with Message and Ask to CoWork buttons.
- Hosts can hand the session to someone else, remove a guest, or make guests view-only.
- Hosting now autosaves every 5 minutes (change it in Settings).
